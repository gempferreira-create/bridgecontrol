About me

Project management, procurement and industry — with experience on both sides of the decision.

I am José Ferreira. My background runs through procurement and operations, estimating and technical development, project management and PMO, always at the junction between client, project team, suppliers and production.

Along the way I have coordinated design, purchasing, suppliers, production, logistics and installation, following projects from requirements and budget through to delivery. That lets me read a quotation beyond its bottom line: what is included, whether it is technically feasible, whether the supplier has the capacity, where the risks sit and whether the lead time is realistic.

Connecting what the project needs to the capacity that can build it.

I do not replace the project team; I step in where decisions need speeding up, alternatives need finding, quotations need structuring and suppliers need keeping aligned, until a clear, buildable solution exists.

Straight questions

What a client usually wants to know.

Why not go straight to the factory?
You can — and often you should. I add value when it is necessary to find alternatives, compare quotations consistently, validate capacity and keep the process moving without pulling the project team into procurement and chasing.
Do you represent a particular factory?
No. I am not tied to any one manufacturer. I start from what the project needs and assess the solution on technical fit, capacity, lead time, location and commercial terms.
I already have suppliers. Does this still make sense?
Yes. I do not need to replace a network that works. I can step in only where there is a gap: a second quotation, extra capacity, a different speciality, a market check or the follow-up of one particular supplier.
What areas do you cover?
The main focus is joinery, bespoke furniture and FF&E, where I have the technical, operational and supplier knowledge. Other trades can be brought in when it makes sense and the right partner exists.
Is everything I share with you kept confidential?
Yes. Documentation received is used only for the agreed scope and shared with potential manufacturers only as far as needed to develop or price the solution. When the project calls for it, I can work under an NDA before receiving sensitive material.
How are you paid?
It depends on the scope. It can be a one-off sourcing fee, a project fee for managing a defined package, or a retainer where the need is recurring. Scope, deliverables and payment are agreed before anything starts.
